# Play Console "Content rating" questionnaire — how to answer it

Like Data safety, this is filled in inside Play Console (**App content → Content
rating**), generated by the IARC (International Age Rating Coalition) questionnaire.
Play uses your answers to assign the actual age ratings (ESRB, PEGI, USK, etc.) shown
on the store listing — you don't pick a rating directly.

## Category
Choose **"Utility, Productivity, Communication, or Other"** (not a game).

## Expected answers
Answer **No** to every content question — none of it applies to CouponKeeper, which
only lets a user log their own promo codes and view a calendar of them:

- Violence (realistic, cartoon, or fantasy) → No
- Sexual content or nudity → No
- Profanity or crude humor → No
- Controlled substances (alcohol/tobacco/drugs) references → No
- Simulated gambling → No (there is no gambling-like mechanic; discounts/coupons are
  not gambling)
- Scary/horror themes → No
- User-generated content shared with other users → No (a coupon's note/code is
  private, local, and never shared with or visible to other users)
- Shares user's location → No (no location permission is requested)
- Allows users to interact/communicate with each other → No (no chat, comments, or
  multiplayer feature exists)
- Allows purchase of digital goods → No (no in-app purchases or store)

## Expected result
With all of the above, the app should land at the lowest rating tier on every system
(e.g. **PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / USK 0**) — appropriate for a general-audience
productivity tool.

## Target age group
When Play separately asks for the **target age group** (under "Target audience and
content" in App content), select an **adult/general audience** group (e.g. 18+ or
"Adults" — whichever the current Play Console UI labels the general, non-child-directed
option), **not** "Primarily child-directed" or "Appeals to children" — CouponKeeper is
a business tool for makers/sellers running promotions, not a kids' app. Answering this
accurately also determines whether Google's Families policies and extra COPPA-style
requirements apply to you (they should not, with these answers).
