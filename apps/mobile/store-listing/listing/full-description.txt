Running promotions across your own site, Etsy, and Instagram means your promo codes end up scattered across notes apps, spreadsheets, and memory. Which ones are still live? Which are about to expire? Which one did you already send your Instagram followers? CouponKeeper keeps every code in one place, so you always know.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Log every promo code with its discount (percent, fixed amount, or free shipping), validity window, and an optional usage limit
• See each code's status at a glance — Not Started, Active, Expiring Soon, or Expired — calculated automatically from its dates
• Assign codes to the channel they belong to — your own site, Etsy, Instagram, or any channel you name and color yourself
• Filter and sort your list by channel, status, discount type, or days remaining
• Browse a monthly calendar with a colored indicator line for every day a code is active, so you can plan campaigns around what's already running
• Get an optional local reminder before a code expires, so nothing quietly lapses
• Attach a short note to any code — an audience, a campaign name, whatever helps you remember why it exists

BUILT FOR MAKERS AND INDEPENDENT SELLERS
CouponKeeper is aimed at people who create and run promotions, not people redeeming them — makers, small shop owners, and independent sellers juggling discounts across more than one storefront.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything you enter stays on your device. There's no account to create, no sign-in, and no backend server — CouponKeeper works fully offline from the moment you install it. Nothing you type is uploaded, shared, or tracked. See the full privacy policy for details.

Light and dark themes, a clean interface, and no ads — just a straightforward way to keep your promo codes organized.
